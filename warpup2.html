Tanks

War Thunder features tanks from WWII to modern times. Famous examples include the German Tiger II and Soviet T‑34. Tank battles emphasize armor, positioning, and teamwork.

Aircraft

Aircraft range from biplanes to supersonic jets. The Spitfire and MiG‑21 are iconic examples. Air combat requires skillful maneuvering and situational awareness.

Naval Vessels

Naval battles include destroyers, cruisers, and even battleships. Players must manage speed, firepower, and positioning to dominate the seas.
