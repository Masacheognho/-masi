The Movies

Dead Poets Society (1989)

Director: Peter Weir
Produced by: Touchstone Pictures
Release Year: 1989

Set in 1959 at the conservative Welton Academy, the film follows English teacher John Keating who inspires his students through poetry to seize the day and think for themselves. His unconventional methods spark excitement but also conflict with the school’s rigid traditions. Read more here.

My Favourite Movie: Dead Poets Society

This film is my favorite because of its powerful message about individuality, courage, and the importance of following one’s passions. Robin Williams’ performance as Mr. Keating is unforgettable, and the story resonates deeply with anyone who has ever struggled against conformity.
