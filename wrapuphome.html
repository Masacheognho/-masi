Welcome to War Thunder

War Thunder is a free‑to‑play vehicular combat multiplayer video game developed by Gaijin Entertainment. Released in 2012, it has grown into one of the most popular military simulators, featuring aircraft, tanks, and naval vessels from multiple eras. Players engage in large‑scale battles across realistic maps, experiencing the thrill of combined arms warfare. Its attention to detail, historical accuracy, and variety of vehicles make it a standout in the genre.
