Game Modes

War Thunder offers three main modes: Arcade, Realistic, and Simulator. Arcade battles are fast‑paced and forgiving, while Realistic battles emphasize historical accuracy and physics. Simulator battles are the most challenging, requiring full immersion and mastery of controls. Each mode caters to different playstyles, ensuring that both casual and hardcore players find enjoyment.

Strategy Tips

Success in War Thunder requires teamwork and situational awareness. Always check your surroundings, coordinate with allies, and use terrain to your advantage. In tank battles, angling your armor can deflect shots. In air combat, energy management is key — climb, dive, and conserve speed. Naval battles demand positioning and timing, as broadside attacks can devastate opponents.
